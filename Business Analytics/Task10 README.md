# Week 10 – Business Analysis using Heat Map

## 📌 Objective
Analyze business performance across different categories using a Heat Map in Tableau.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Tableau Public
- CSV Dataset
- GitHub

## 📊 Visualizations
- Heat Map – Sales by Department and City
- Bar Chart – Monthly Sales by Department
- Pie Chart – Sales Contribution by City
- Line Chart – Monthly Sales Trend

## 🎛️ Filter
- Department Filter

## 💡 Business Insights
- Sales performance varies across departments and cities.
- Some department-city combinations show higher sales contribution.
- The Heat Map helps identify high and low performing areas.
- Department filtering makes comparison easier.

## 💼 Business Recommendations
- Focus resources on high-performing departments and cities.
- Develop targeted strategies to improve low-performing areas.

## ✅ Conclusion
The Tableau dashboard provides a clear view of business performance across departments and cities. The Heat Map helps identify high and low performing areas and supports better business analysis.
