## Business Insights

Insight 1
South Indian cuisine has the highest number of restaurants in the dataset.

Insight 2
Most restaurants have ratings between 3.8 and 4.4, indicating generally good customer satisfaction.

Insight 3
Bakery and dessert restaurants receive some of the highest customer ratings.

Insight 4
Restaurant prices vary across cuisines, but higher prices do not always result in higher ratings.

Insight 5
The dashboard helps identify popular cuisines and highly rated restaurants, supporting better business decisions.
