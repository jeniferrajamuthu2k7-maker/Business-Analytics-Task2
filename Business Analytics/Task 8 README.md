To analyze the contribution of different business categories using a Tree Map in Tableau.

Dataset
Statistics Practice Dataset – 2000 Records

Tool
Tableau Public

Visualizations

Bar Chart – Monthly Sales by Department
Line Chart – Monthly Sales Trend
Pie Chart – Monthly Sales Contribution by City
Key Insights
Highest sales department
Lowest sales department
Major sales-contributing categories
Highest contributing city
Sales trend observations
Recommendations
Focus resources on high-performing departments.
Improve low-performing departments and cities.
