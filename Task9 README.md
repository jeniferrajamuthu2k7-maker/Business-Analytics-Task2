## 💡 Business Insights

1. **Monthly Sales Fluctuation:**  
   Monthly sales vary throughout the year, reaching the highest level in April before declining during the following months.

2. **Highest and Lowest Period:**  
   April recorded the highest monthly sales of **35,790**, while September recorded the lowest at **24,787**.

3. **Department Performance:**  
   The **IT department** generated the highest sales at **90,303**, closely followed by the **Sales department** at **89,581**.

4. **City Performance:**  
   **Chennai** recorded the highest city-level sales at **47,655**, followed by Jaipur and Bengaluru.

5. **Experience and Sales:**  
   The scatter plot shows that there is no simple linear relationship between employee experience and monthly sales. Most employees are concentrated at lower sales levels, with a few high-sales outliers.


## 📌 Business Recommendations

1. **Improve Lower-Performing Areas:**  
   Analyze lower-performing departments and cities to identify opportunities for training, resource allocation, and targeted sales initiatives.

2. **Address Sales Fluctuations:**  
   Investigate the factors behind monthly sales fluctuations and develop targeted strategies for periods with lower sales performance.
