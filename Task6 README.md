# Business Trend Analysis Dashboard

Tableau Link: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/jenifer.r7465/viz/week6_17878992544390/BUSINESSTRENDANALYSISDASHBOARD

## Business Insights

- The Area Chart shows changes in sales over time and helps identify important sales trends.
- Sales performance varies across different product categories.
- Some periods show higher sales, indicating stronger business performance.
- Regional sales performance differs, with some regions contributing more to overall sales.
- Profit varies across product categories, helping identify more profitable areas of the business.

## Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing product categories to increase overall sales.
- Plan inventory based on periods of higher customer demand.
- Improve marketing strategies during low-sales periods.
- Analyze regional performance to identify opportunities for business growth.
- Focus on products and categories that generate higher profit.

## Conclusion

This Tableau dashboard successfully analyzes business performance and sales trends over time. The Area Chart provides a clear view of how sales change across different periods, while the additional visualizations help compare category, regional, and profit performance. These insights can help businesses improve sales planning, inventory management, marketing strategies, and overall decision-making.
