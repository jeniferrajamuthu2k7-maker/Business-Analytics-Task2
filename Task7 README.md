# Week 7 – Business Trend Analysis using Scatter Plot

## Objective
To analyze business performance using a Scatter Plot in Tableau and identify important trends and relationships.

## Dataset
Statistics Practice Dataset – 2000 records

## Tool Used
- Tableau Public
- CSV Dataset

## Visualizations

### 1. Scatter Plot
Monthly Sales vs Performance Score

### 2. Bar Chart
Monthly Sales by Department

### 3. Line Chart
Monthly Sales Trend

### 4. Tree Chart
Monthly Sales by City

## Key Analysis
The Scatter Plot shows the relationship between Monthly Sales and Performance Score. 
The trend line helps understand whether higher performance scores are associated with higher monthly sales.

## Conclusion
The dashboard provides a visual analysis of business performance and helps identify relationships between employee performance, sales, departments and cities.
